# ReSee — Product Vision

## What is ReSee?

ReSee is a career-readiness platform that takes a person from "I don't know
where to start" to "I applied, interviewed, and got the offer" inside one
product. It unifies structured learning, topic-based practice and mock
tests, an AI-templated resume builder, a mock interview simulator, and a
job board with application tracking — all reading from one shared profile,
so progress in one module informs every other.

## Why was it created?

The career-prep journey today is fragmented across a course platform, a
separate resume tool, a separate mock-interview app, a separate job board,
and no single place that connects them. A user's course completion never
informs their resume; their resume never informs which jobs they're
matched to. ReSee was built to close that loop — one profile, one skill
graph, one continuous journey from learning to hired.

## Target users

Primarily early-to-mid-career job seekers in tech and adjacent fields:
final-year students, fresh graduates, professionals with 1–5+ years of
experience looking to move up or switch tracks, and career switchers
entering tech from an unrelated background. Recruiters and colleges are
explicitly future personas — no B2B surface exists yet. Full persona
detail: `docs/product/prd.md` §2.

## Problems it solves

- **Fragmentation** — one account instead of four disconnected tools.
- **Generic resumes** — template selection is driven by the user's actual
  profile (fresher vs. experienced), not a blank gallery.
- **Untested interview readiness** — role- and experience-level-specific
  mock interviews before the real one.
- **Opaque progress** — a single Career Score and skill graph instead of
  scattered course-completion percentages.

## Product vision

Every action a user takes anywhere in the product — finishing a lesson,
acing a practice topic, updating a resume, completing a mock interview —
feeds one continuously updating picture of their career readiness, and
that picture actively points them at their next best action.

## Long-term mission

Become the default operating system for career readiness: the place a
student opens on day one of their job search and doesn't leave until
they've been placed — and the place they return to for the next move in
their career after that.
